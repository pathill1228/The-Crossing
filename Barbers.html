Benji

My name is Benjamin Gonzalez, and I’ve been cutting hair since I was 15 years old. What started as something I did for friends quickly turned into a craft I fell in love with. Over the years, I’ve dedicated myself to mastering clean fades, beard work, and modern men’s cuts that bring out each client’s best look.

I take pride in precision and consistency. Every cut I do is intentional — it’s not just about the haircut, it’s about giving someone the confidence to walk out feeling sharp, refreshed, and put together.

My goal with every client is simple: listen, deliver, and elevate. Whether it’s a maintenance cut or a full transformation, I make sure my chair is a place where people feel taken care of and leave looking their absolute best.

I’m excited to continue growing here and to keep providing the kind of work that speaks for itself.

Lyndon

With 10 years of experience behind the chair, Lyndon has built a reputation for clean fades, sharp beard work, and modern, confidence-boosting styles. His passion for barbering goes beyond the cut—every client in his chair gets genuine conversation, attention to detail, and a personalized experience.

A dedicated father of three sons, Lyndon brings the same commitment and care to his craft as he does to his family. He’s also an active member of the Burleson community, known for his people-first attitude and his drive to make everyone who walks through his doors feel welcome.

Whether you’re looking for a fresh fade, a perfectly sculpted beard, or a bold new look, Lyndon brings skill, heart, and professionalism to every service.
